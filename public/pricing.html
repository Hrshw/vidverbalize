How many clips can be generated?

That is dependent on the length and content of your video,for e.g from an hour long video it can generate anything between 30-45 shorts depending on the content.

how much many minutes do I get ?

On the free plan you get 75 minutes of processing that refreshes every month , 150 minutes on the starter plan, 300 on the pro and 500 on the pro+. On the yearly plans you get all the minutes at once, so thats 3600 on the pro annual and 6000 minutes on the Pro + annual

I am beginner can I user it?

Most definitely! you can check out our YouTube channel https://www.youtube.com/@vidyoai and our website's blog or knowledge base https://vidyoaihelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us to get started.

what does vidyo.ai do?

vidyo.ai is Content Repurposing AI Tool. That allows you to upload your long form videos and automatically picks out the best clips and offers you a range of customizations

Is my data safe with you?

Yes, we prioritize data security. We use advanced measures to protect your information and never share it with unauthorized parties. Your data is safe with us.

Who is it for?

Content repurposing is super critical for businesses who do content marketing, video creators, podcasters & agencies.

Can I make custom template?

Yes, you can create custom templates! This feature is available on our paid plans, allowing you to tailor templates to your specific needs and branding. Unlock this capability by upgrading to one of our paid plans.

How are the minutes calculated?

Minutes are calculated based on the length of your uploaded video. Our AI analyzes and processes the entire video, making the minutes used correspond to its duration. So, the longer the video, the more minutes are consumed.

What other features do you have?

CutMagic: Conquer Scene Changes ,Quick Captions/Hashtags: Click and Voila! ,Social Preview: Get Platform-Perfect Previews,Brand Kit: Dress Your Videos Your Way, Drafts: Edit at Your Own Pace

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?

Absolutely! You have the flexibility to upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Simply navigate to the "Manage Subscription" section in your account settings, and you'll find options to adjust your plan according to your preferences and requirements.

In what resolution will the download files be in?

Free users can download files in 720p resolution, while paid users have access to download files in high-definition 1080p resolution.

What languages are supported?

We currently support English and will soon be adding Spanish, German, and French languages in the upcoming weeks to cater to a wider audience.

How much do the paid plans cost?

The pricing for our paid plans varies based on the level of features and support you need. You can find a detailed breakdown of each plan and their costs on the "Pricing" page of our website https://vidyo.ai/pricing. Don't hesitate to reach out if you need help selecting a plan that suits your needs best.

How to cancel my subscription?

To cancel your subscription, simply navigate to "Manage Subscription" in your account settings and click on "Cancel Subscription". Please feel free to reach out to us if you need any assistance during this process.

How to upgrade my account/subscription to Pro or Pro+?

To upgrade your account to Starter ,Pro or Pro+, go to your account settings and look for the "Upgrade" button. From there, you'll be able to choose between monthly or annual plans for either Starter, Pro or Pro+. Select the plan that suits your needs and follow the prompts to complete the upgrade process.

I need extra minutes, how do to top-up?

The top-up feature is currently exclusive to Starters, PRO and PRO+ users. To add extra minutes, simply click on the minute balance on your home screen or use the "Upgrade" tab. From there, you can select your preferred plan to add more minutes to your account. It's that easy!

Do you have enterprise plans?

Yes, we do offer enterprise plans for customers requiring more than 500 minutes per month. Please email us at [email protected] for more information and we'll be more than happy to assist you with your enterprise needs.
